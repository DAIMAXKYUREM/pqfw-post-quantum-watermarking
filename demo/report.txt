MINISTRY OF SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT
INTERNAL ASSESSMENT NOTE -- RESTRICTED CIRCULATION

Subject: Procurement review, financial year 2025-26, second quarter.

This note is released to the distribution list at annexe A and to no other
reader. It summarises findings from the quarterly review of tender processes
across the four regional offices, together with the recommendations of the
review committee and a provisional timetable for remedial action.

FINDINGS

One. Three of the fourteen tenders examined were awarded without a documented
comparison against the published evaluation criteria. In each case the file
contains a recommendation note but no scoring sheet. The absence of a scoring
sheet does not by itself indicate irregularity, but it removes the ability to
demonstrate that the award was made on the stated basis.

Two. The average interval between the closing date and the award decision was
sixty one days, against a service standard of thirty. Two awards exceeded one
hundred days. Delay of this order materially advantages incumbent suppliers,
who continue to be paid under extension arrangements while a decision is
pending.

Three. Two suppliers appear under slightly different registered names in the
vendor master and were treated as distinct entities during evaluation. The
review committee was not able to establish whether this affected the outcome
of either tender, and recommends that the vendor master be deduplicated before
the next cycle opens.

RECOMMENDATIONS

The committee recommends that scoring sheets be made a mandatory attachment at
the point of award, that a standing exception report be produced for any award
exceeding the service standard by more than fifty per cent, and that the vendor
master be reconciled against the register of companies annually.

A response to these recommendations is requested from each regional office
within thirty working days of the date of this note. Recipients are reminded
that this document is individually accountable to the person named on the
distribution list and that its onward transmission, in whole or in part, is a
breach of the terms under which it was released.
